# Playbook PB-024 — Deliberate Engineering Practice & Continuous Skill Acquisition SOP

## Objective
Provide a daily operational protocol for senior engineers to maintain cutting-edge mastery, eliminate knowledge decay, and systematically master new architectural patterns.

---

## 1. The Daily 45-Minute Mastery Habit Loop

```
[ Phase 1: Active Recall (10 mins) ]
  - Review 5 cards from the Spaced Repetition Flashcard Deck.
  - Explain the concept out loud from first principles before flipping.
             │
[ Phase 2: Break-and-Fix Drill (25 mins) ]
  - Pick 1 issue from REACT_ISSUES_LAB_INDEX.md.
  - Reproduce and fix the issue in a clean sandbox.
  - Write an automated regression test in Vitest.
             │
[ Phase 3: Journal & Synthesis (10 mins) ]
  - Record the core architectural takeaway and code diff in the Daily Learning Journal.
```

---

## 2. Weekly Knowledge Gap Audit

- Every Friday, audit the master tracking indices (`REACT_ISSUES_LAB_INDEX.md` and `DEBUGGING_PLAYBOOKS_INDEX.md`).
- Select the 2 least familiar playbooks and execute full simulated triage scenarios in staging.
